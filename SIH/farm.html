How the sack fills up / यह कैसे काम करता है

1. Owner declares a route

A vehicle already travelling shares its date, route and free capacity.

2. Farmers request space

Farmers along the way see the free kilograms and pickup point, and ask to join.

3. Sack fills, fare is fixed

The fare rule the owner chose decides what every farmer pays — no bargaining.

4. SMS confirms the trip

Both sides get an SMS with pickup time and delivery date — no app data needed.
